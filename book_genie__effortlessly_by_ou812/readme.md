# Book Genie: Your AI Writing Assistant

Welcome to Book Genie, an advanced AI-powered application designed to help you effortlessly create complete books from a simple idea. Whether you're an author battling writer's block, a creator looking to generate narrative content, or an educator crafting stories, Book Genie provides the tools to turn your concepts into fully-fledged books, chapter by chapter.

This application leverages cutting-edge AI for text and image generation, combined with a rich user interface that offers deep customization, real-time collaboration, and multiple ways to experience and share your creations.

## Key Features

- **AI-Powered Content Creation**: Generate entire books, including titles and chapter content, from a simple prompt.
- **Deep Customization**: Control every aspect of your book, from genre and writing style to narrative structure and chapter length.
- **Interactive Editing**: Refine your book with chapter-specific tools, including text editing, AI-powered revisions, and image integration.
- **Multiple UI Themes & Fonts**: Personalize your writing environment with various themes like Dark Mode, Sepia, and even Neon styles.
- **Save, Load & Export**: Save your work as a JSON file to continue later. Export your finished book to PDF, HTML, or TXT.
- **Real-Time Collaboration**: Work with others on your book idea in real-time with shared cursors and live text updates.
- **Community Library**: Publish your creations and explore books written by others in an interactive 3D library.
- **AI Librarian Chatbot**: Get help and book recommendations in the Community Library from a friendly AI assistant.
- **Interactive Flip Book**: Experience your generated content in an immersive 3D flip-book interface.
- **Text-to-Speech (TTS)**: Listen to your chapters with configurable AI-generated narration.

## How to Use

### 1. Starting Your Book

- **Main Input**: Begin by typing your book idea, topic, outline, or even just a single word into the main text area.
- **Language Selection**: Choose the desired language for your book. The AI will write in the selected language.
- **Analyze & Improve**: Click the **🧠 (Analyze & Improve)** icon inside the text area. The AI will provide a critique of your prompt and suggest three enhanced versions to help you get better results.

### 2. Generating Chapters

- **Settings**: Click the **Settings** button to configure your book.
    - **Generation Tab**: Set the number of chapters, desired chapter length, genre, and writing style.
    - **Story Details Tab**: For more control, define specific narrative elements like conflict type, story structure (beginning, middle, end), core themes, or even ask the AI to write in the style of a famous author.
- **Generate**: Once you're ready, click the **Generate Chapters** button. Book Genie will first create a book title and a table of contents. Then, it will write the content for each chapter sequentially. A progress bar will show the status of the generation.

### 3. Editing and Refining

Each generated chapter comes with a suite of tools for refinement:

- **Edit (📝)**: Manually edit the chapter's title and text content.
- **Regenerate (🔄)**: Re-write the entire chapter content using the original title and settings.
- **AI Command (🤖)**: Open a prompt to give the AI specific instructions to revise the chapter (e.g., "make this more dramatic," "add a description of the main character," "translate this chapter to French").
- **Add Image (🖼️)**: Generate and insert images into the chapter based on a prompt.
- **Text-to-Speech (🔊)**: Listen to an AI-narrated version of the chapter.
- **Bookmark (🔖)**: Set a bookmark on a chapter. You can quickly jump back to it using the "Go to Bookmark" button in the main menu.
- **Collapse/Expand**: Hide or show chapter content to keep your workspace organized.

### 4. Saving, Loading, and Exporting

- **Save**: Click the **Save** button to download a `.json` file containing your entire book, including all content and settings. The application also automatically saves your progress to your browser's local storage.
- **Load**: Click the **Load** button and select a previously saved `.json` file to continue your work.
- **Export**: Click the **Export** button to open a menu where you can download your book as a formatted **PDF**, a standalone **HTML** file, or a plain **TXT** file.

## Advanced Features

### Multiplayer Collaboration

Work on your book's initial concept with friends or colleagues. When multiple users are in the same session, their avatars will appear at the top. You will see their cursors and text edits in the main input area in real-time.

### Interactive Flip Book Generator

Click the **Interactive Book Generator** button to switch to a different interface. Here, you can enter a prompt and generate a book that is immediately displayed in an immersive 3D flip-book view, allowing you to turn the pages virtually.

### UI Customization

In **Settings → UI & Style**, you can:
- **Change the UI Theme**: Choose from over 10 themes, including Light, Dark, Sepia, and Hacker Neon.
- **Select a Font**: Customize the application's font.
- **Regenerate the Cover**: The AI generates a cover image for your book that is used as the page background. You can refine this image by entering a new prompt (e.g., "add a castle in the background") and regenerating it.

## Community Library

### Browsing and Sharing

- **Navigate**: Click the **Community Library** button to visit the shared bookshelf.
- **3D Bookshelf**: Browse books published by other users in a unique 3D interface. Click on a book to view its details.
- **Load & Download**: You can load any community book directly into the generator to read or remix it, or download its source `.json` file.

### Publishing Your Work

1.  Save your book from the main application to get the `.json` file.
2.  Go to the **Community Library** page.
3.  In the "Publish Your Book" section, choose your saved `.json` file.
4.  Click **Publish**. Your book will be uploaded and will appear on the 3D bookshelf for others to discover.

### AI Librarian

While in the Community Library, click the **🤖 (AI Librarian)** icon to open a chat window. You can ask the AI for book recommendations, search for books by title or author, or ask questions about the books available on the shelf.
